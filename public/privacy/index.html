Privacy information

Quote requests

We use the contact details, locations, and load information you provide to respond to your enquiry and arrange a job with you. Quote requests are stored through Netlify Forms and may be sent to our notification inbox.

Address suggestions and maps

City suggestions are bundled with this website. Street searches and the coordinates used for a route preview are sent to Geoapify. OpenFreeMap supplies the map background using OpenStreetMap data and receives map requests from your browser. Use my location asks for your browser’s permission first. You can enter your location manually instead.

Geoapify privacy information · OpenFreeMap privacy information · Netlify privacy information

Reviews

We receive the feedback and contact details you choose to submit. We publish feedback and your public name only when you give permission. Email addresses stay private. Contact us if you would like us to review or remove feedback you submitted.

Drafts on your device

The quote form keeps a draft in your browser tab for up to two hours, so you can return after a refresh. The draft includes entered details and selected locations. Start over clears the form. A successful submission clears the draft. Draft storage may be unavailable in some browser settings.

Admin sign-in and workspace preferences

The admin workspace uses secure sign-in cookies to keep authorized admins signed in. Workspace sessions expire after 30 minutes of inactivity and have a maximum duration of 12 hours. Personal workspace settings are saved to the admin account. Notification read status and image display preferences are also stored in the browser.

Questions or data requests

Email RS on the Go to ask about information you submitted, request a correction, or request deletion.
